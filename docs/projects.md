## Commerce and Payment Platform

**Focus:** marketplace interfaces and online commerce workflows.

I contributed to a marketplace and payment platform. This showcase contains simplified Python examples adapted from private backend work:

- [Wallet transfers](../src/showcase/wallet.py): balance handling, transfer validation, and spending limits.
- [Payment requests](../src/showcase/payments.py): provider rules, fees, phone formatting, and request metadata.
- [Shop reporting](../src/showcase/reporting.py): monthly sales and product metrics.
- [Message encryption](../src/showcase/message_crypto.py): a small Fernet helper with documented demo limitations.

The [walkthroughs](python-examples.md) explain how these examples relate to the original Django service-layer scenarios. The public modules are standalone demonstrations, not the complete application.

**Access:** private application; public examples linked above.
